Baby,

Happy 24th birthday! I wanted to give you something you can't put in a vase, so... I made you a whole website. Yes, I'm aware. You're welcome.

This is your first birthday with me, and I can't believe how lucky I am to get to celebrate it.

Can I tell you something I don't think I've ever said properly? You are my first girlfriend. Ever. Before you, I didn't really know what any of this felt like: waiting for one person's message, smiling at my phone like an idiot, wanting to tell someone about every small thing in my day. You were my first for all of it. And I'll be honest with you, oun: I hope you're also my last. I don't want to learn how to love anyone else. I just want to keep learning how to love you better.

It all started with one simple text on Instagram. I still think about that sometimes. One small message, and somehow it turned into you, into us, into the best thing that has ever happened to me.

We've only been us since February, but somehow it already feels like I've known you way longer, in the best way. You're the first person I want to tell everything to. You make photo booths more fun, fancy dinners funnier, and the most normal days feel like something worth taking a picture of (as you can see, I took a lot).

I love you when you laugh. I love you when you complain in that cute little voice. I love you when you study with that serious face, when you try your best, and when you finally get it. Honestly, I love you just for existing, my cheeky cheeks dumpling.

I still think about our date at Khema. The city lights, the red roses, and you. If I could replay one night forever, it would be that one.

I also want to say sorry. For the days I couldn't make you happy, and for the times I wasn't the boyfriend you deserved. I know they happened, and I'm not going to pretend they didn't. You're my first, so I'm still learning, and sometimes I learn slowly. But I promise I'm trying, every single day, to be better for you. Thank you for being patient with me through all of it, and for not giving up on me when I got it wrong.

And I'm sorry we're so far apart. Tokyo to Phnom Penh, 4,408 km, two hours apart. I hate that so much of us happens through a screen, that I can't just show up with flowers or hug you after a long day, or sit next to you and steal your food. Some nights that distance feels really heavy. But every time I see your face on a call, I'm reminded why it's worth it. I really believe next year can be different for us. I'm holding on to that, and to you.

through a screen, still my favorite view

Can I tell you a secret? I'm crying a little bit while I make this. Just knowing I can't be next to you whenever I want, on your birthday of all days. But it's worth it, baby. You're worth it. These are tears of love, and I'm happy to cry every single one of them for you.

Thank you for laughing at my jokes, even the bad ones, for your hugs, for your patience, for every "goodnight" and every "did you eat yet?", and for being so completely, unapologetically you.

I hope 24 is soft and loud and full of everything that makes you happy. I hope it's kind to you, because you deserve kindness more than anyone I know. And whatever this year brings, good or hard, I want you to know you won't face any of it alone. Not while I'm here.

You were my first. I hope you'll be my only. And this is only the beginning, oun: the first birthday of a hundred more that we get to spend together.

Happy birthday, baby. I love you, more than this whole website can say.

Always yours 🤍
