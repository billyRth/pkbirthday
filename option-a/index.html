Baby,

Happy 24th birthday! I wanted to give you something you can't put in a vase, so... I made you a whole website. Yes, I'm aware. You're welcome.

This is your first birthday with me, and I can't believe how lucky I am to get to celebrate it.

We've only been us since February, but somehow it already feels like I've known you way longer, in the best way. You're the first person I want to tell everything to. You make photo booths more fun, fancy dinners funnier, and the most normal days feel like something worth taking a picture of (as you can see, I took a lot).

I also want to say sorry. For the days I couldn't make you happy, and for the times I wasn't the boyfriend you deserved. I know they happened, and I'm still learning how to be better for you. Thank you for being patient with me through all of it.

And I'm sorry we're so far apart. I hate that so much of us happens through a screen, that I can't just show up with flowers or hug you after a long day. But I really believe next year can be different for us. I'm holding on to that, and to you.

Thank you for laughing at my jokes, even the bad ones, for your hugs, and for being so completely, unapologetically you.

I hope 24 is soft and loud and full of everything that makes you happy. And this is only the beginning, oun: the first birthday of a hundred more that we get to spend together.

Happy birthday, baby. I love you.

Always yours 🤍
